Debugging a desk

  • Run at the problem desk, then somewhere the Wi-Fi is known to be good, and compare.
  • Let it run 15+ minutes; drops are usually intermittent.
  • Sit where the person sits; a body or a monitor arm costs several dB.
  • This measures your adapter. If yours is fine and theirs isn't, suspect their machine or dock.
